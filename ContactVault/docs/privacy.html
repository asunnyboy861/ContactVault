Privacy Policy for ContactVault

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Introduction

ContactVault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Data Collection

ContactVault is designed with privacy as a core principle. We want to be clear about what data we collect:

  • Contact Data: Your contacts are accessed locally on your device only for the purpose of creating backups. We never transmit your contact data to any external server.
  • Backup Files: All backup files are stored locally on your device. Encrypted backups use AES-256 encryption and are only accessible with your password.
  • No Analytics: We do not collect any usage analytics, tracking data, or personal information.
  • No Third-Party Services: We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Data Storage

All data processed by ContactVault remains on your device. Backup files are stored in your device's local file system. When you choose to share or export backups, the data is transferred only through iOS system sharing mechanisms directly under your control.

Encryption

When you enable encryption for your backups, ContactVault uses AES-256 encryption to protect your data. The encryption password is never stored by the app. If you forget your password, encrypted backups cannot be recovered.

Permissions

ContactVault requests the following permissions:

  • Contacts: Required to read and backup your contact information.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: Optional, used to authenticate before accessing encrypted backups.
  • Notifications: Optional, used to send backup reminders if you enable them.

Children's Privacy

ContactVault does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Our app does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
