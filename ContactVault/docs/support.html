Frequently Asked Questions

How do I backup my contacts?

Open the Backup tab, grant contact access permission, select your preferred format (VCF or CSV), optionally enable encryption, and tap "Backup Now".

What is the difference between VCF and CSV formats?

VCF (vCard) is the standard format for contact data and can be imported by most contact apps. CSV is a spreadsheet format that works well with Excel and Google Sheets for viewing and editing.

How does encryption work?

When you enable encryption, your backup is protected with AES-256 encryption using your chosen password. Only someone with the password can decrypt and access the backup data.

I forgot my encryption password. Can I recover my backup?

Unfortunately, if you forget your encryption password, the encrypted backup cannot be recovered. We recommend storing your passwords in a secure password manager.

Does ContactVault send my contacts to a server?

No. ContactVault processes all data locally on your device. Your contacts never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export them.

How do I restore contacts from a backup?

Go to the Restore tab, select a backup file from your history, and tap the share button to open it in the iOS Contacts app or any compatible app.
